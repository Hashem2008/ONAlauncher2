Complementary: compute-only options off on phones (2026-11-09 d)

User log: the ONA Boost 1.1.0 Iris fix works ("compute shaders and images off for shader packs").
Next stop: "Shader storage buffers/immutable buffer storage is not supported on this graphics card,
however the shaderpack requested them?". Complementary's shaders.properties declares bufferObject.0
(50 MB .. 810 MB) and bufferObject.3 when WORLD_SPACE_REFLECTIONS > 0 (with COLORED_LIGHTING,
WORLD_SPACE_PLAYER_REF), without checking the SSBO feature; the profile in use had them on.
New game/graphics/ShaderPackCompat (called at launch after ONA Graphics / Shader Community): for the
active pack, when SmartRenderer.gl43Safe(pack) is false (no compute on the engine), the options
COLORED_LIGHTING=0, WORLD_SPACE_REFLECTIONS=-1, WORLD_SPACE_PLAYER_REF=-1 are written to the pack's
options file if the pack declares them and they are on. Everything else stays. SSBO stays hidden on
the engine (an 810 MB buffer would not fit a phone anyway).
